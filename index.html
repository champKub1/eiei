A legacy beyond time

For nine generations the museum has kept watch over works that outlived their makers: pigments ground by hand, marble cut by lamplight, gold beaten thinner than breath.

Our mission is to conserve these objects, study them honestly, and open the hall to anyone who walks in with curiosity.
